Declare storage: `supports_table_storage_introspection` answers `True` and `total_bytes` fills `size_bytes`, while `data_size_bytes` and `index_size_bytes` stay `None` because parts and sparse skipping indices are not a row-plus-index shape. The three row families -- profiles, samples and coverage -- aggregate the rows exactly, one scan per column, flagged `source='aggregate'`, and `ClickHouseColumnDataProfile.runtime_types` is a named backend field rather than an `extra` key. Six operational families answer `False` and exit 2 because this backend has no collector, dependency graph or native DDL. Engine functions go through `_engine_function`, the non-existent `typeof` is corrected to `toTypeName`, and `groupUniqArray` and `currentUser` are reached the same way. Sixteen CLI types derive from `_TYPE_CAPABILITIES` with one `_NAME_RULE`, and four new flags are refused where they cannot be read.
