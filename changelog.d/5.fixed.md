Refuse the constraints whose silent outcome is worse than the error. `UNIQUE (col)` is a `SYNTAX_ERROR` on the server, and an unnamed `FOREIGN KEY (...) REFERENCES ...` is *accepted and silently discarded* — measured on 26.7.3.19, the DDL succeeds, `SHOW CREATE TABLE` reports no foreign key, `system.tables.create_table_query` does not contain the string, an orphan row inserts and reads back fine, and dropping the referenced parent succeeds as well. Emitting either would produce a schema with no constraint and nothing in the server's metadata to explain the missing enforcement, so `mixins/ddl_column.py` raises `UnsupportedFeatureError` at the point of declaration with that measured reason attached.
