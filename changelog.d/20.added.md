Add real ClickHouse materialized view support: `ClickHouseCreateMaterializedViewExpression` covering both ClickHouse flavours (incremental with `TO <table>` or a mandatory `ENGINE`, plus `POPULATE`; and refreshable with `REFRESH EVERY|AFTER`, `OFFSET`, `RANDOMIZE FOR`, `DEPENDS ON`, `SETTINGS`, `APPEND [INCREMENTAL]` and `EMPTY`), `ClickHouseDropMaterializedViewExpression` (`DROP VIEW`), `ClickHouseRefreshMaterializedViewExpression` (`SYSTEM REFRESH VIEW`, optionally `SYSTEM WAIT VIEW`) and `ClickHouseModifyMaterializedViewRefreshExpression` (`ALTER TABLE ... MODIFY REFRESH`). Replaces the previous state where `supports_materialized_view()` reported `False` because the SQL-standard form could not be rendered. Server-side rules are validated in the constructor *and* the formatter: `ENGINE`/`TO` is mandatory, `REFRESH` needs at least one of `EVERY`/`AFTER`/`DEPENDS ON`, `OFFSET` requires `EVERY`, `APPEND INCREMENTAL` requires `APPEND`, and `POPULATE` conflicts with `REFRESH`.
